# NovaMart Australia — Orders and Payments Policy

## Accepted payment methods

NovaMart accepts:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- NovaMart gift cards

Payment is charged when the order is confirmed.

## Order confirmation

After placing an order, customers receive a confirmation email containing their order number and purchase details.

Customers should check their spam or junk folder if the email does not arrive within 30 minutes.

## Cancelling an order

Customers may request cancellation before the order has been packed.

Once an order has been packed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled. The customer may return eligible products after delivery under the Returns and Refunds Policy.

Cancellation requests are not guaranteed because orders may be processed quickly.

## Changing an order or delivery address

Products cannot be added to or removed from a confirmed order.

Customers may request a delivery-address change before the order is packed. NovaMart cannot guarantee address changes after processing begins.

## Failed payments

If payment fails, customers should:

- Confirm that their card information is correct
- Check that sufficient funds are available
- Contact their bank
- Try another accepted payment method

NovaMart cannot access the reason a bank declines a transaction.

## Duplicate or pending charges

A pending charge may be a temporary authorisation and usually disappears within 3–5 business days.

Customers who see two completed charges for the same order should contact NovaMart Support with their order number and payment details. Customers should not send their full card number or security code.
