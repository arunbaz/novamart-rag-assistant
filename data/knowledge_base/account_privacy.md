# NovaMart Australia — Account and Privacy Support

## Creating an account

Customers may create a NovaMart account using their name, email address and a secure password.

An account is optional. Customers may also place orders using guest checkout.

## Forgotten passwords

Customers who forget their password should select “Forgot password” on the sign-in page.

NovaMart will send a password-reset link to the registered email address. The link expires after 30 minutes.

Customers should check their spam or junk folder if the email does not arrive.

## Updating account details

Signed-in customers can update their:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Saved delivery addresses

For security reasons, customers cannot change the email address connected to an order that has already been placed.

## Locked accounts

An account may be temporarily locked after five unsuccessful sign-in attempts.

Customers should wait 15 minutes before trying again or reset their password.

## Personal information

NovaMart uses customer information to process orders, provide support, prevent fraud and improve its services.

NovaMart does not sell customers’ personal information.

## Requesting account deletion

Customers may request account deletion by contacting NovaMart Support from their registered email address.

NovaMart may retain transaction records when required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or warranty purposes.

Account deletion requests are normally completed within 30 calendar days.
