# NovaMart Australia — Product Warranty Policy

## Warranty period

Most NovaMart products include a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date.

Products with a different warranty period will clearly show that information on their product page and receipt.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or manufacturing, including:

- Products that stop working during normal use
- Faulty electrical components
- Manufacturing defects
- Missing or defective components included with the product

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover:

- Accidental damage
- Normal wear and tear
- Water damage unless the product is advertised as waterproof
- Damage caused by misuse or incorrect installation
- Unauthorised repairs or modifications
- Lost or stolen products

## Making a warranty claim

Customers should contact NovaMart Support and provide:

- Their order number
- A description of the problem
- Photos or a short video showing the fault
- The product serial number, when applicable

NovaMart may offer a repair, replacement or refund after assessing the claim.

Customers should not return a faulty product before receiving return instructions from NovaMart Support.

## Australian Consumer Law

NovaMart warranties are provided in addition to customers’ rights under Australian Consumer Law.
